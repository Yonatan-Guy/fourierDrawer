the first terms, written out

Every epicycle is one cosine in x and one sine in y. These are the biggest, in order.

how fast the terms shrink

Amplitude against term number, both on log scales. The slope is the shape's smoothness: gentle curves fall away fast, corners decay like 1/k, cusps like 1/k².

how much you actually need

Share of the total amplitude captured by the first n arms. Where this flattens, the slider stops changing anything you can see.
